# 5. WORLD DESIGN – VOLPIANO, ITALY

## Core requirement

The game must take place in a stylized, cartoon recreation of the real town of Volpiano, in the Metropolitan City of Turin, Piedmont, Italy.

This is a critical project requirement.

DO NOT generate a generic fictional Italian town as the final game environment.

The goal is to create a miniature cartoon interpretation of the real Volpiano, recognizable to someone who lives there.

## Main journey

Departure:
**Via Trento, Volpiano, Italy — exact departure configured locally.**

Destination:
**School area on Via Trieste, Volpiano, Italy**

The exact school entrance and legal road access must be verified before final route generation.

The main route must be based on the real geographic road network between these locations.

The starting property should use a simplified, generic cartoon house initially, not an automatically inferred reconstruction of the private building.

Keep private addresses in local development configuration, rather than public repositories or distributed source data.

## Geographic data

Use OpenStreetMap as the primary geographic reference.

Investigate the available OSM data for:
- Streets
- Intersections
- Road directionality
- Roundabouts
- Pedestrian crossings
- Building footprints
- Parks
- Playgrounds
- Public squares
- Schools
- Important landmarks

Build an appropriate OSM import or conversion pipeline.

Document licensing and attribution requirements for OSM and any third-party assets.

Maintain actual road topology whenever practical.

Simplify geometry while keeping intersections and spatial relationships recognizable.

Do not invent street geometry when reliable data is available.

If accurate source data is unavailable, use clearly documented temporary placeholders.

## Geographic coordinate conversion

Implement a suitable conversion from latitude/longitude to local Godot coordinates.

Use a local origin near the playable area to avoid unnecessary floating-point precision problems.

Keep the following configurable:
- Geographic origin
- Map scale
- Road width
- Building height approximation
- Terrain elevation
- Spawn locations
- School arrival zone

The environment must support future map expansion.
